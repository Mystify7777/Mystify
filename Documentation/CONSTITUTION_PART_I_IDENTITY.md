# THE MYSTIFY OBSERVATORY CONSTITUTION — PART I: IDENTITY

This Part establishes the Observatory's identity: what it is, how its interpretations relate, and the environment that contains all sectors.

## Article 1 — Nature
- The Observatory is the product: the environment itself is the primary artifact.
- Applications are sectors inhabiting the product; sectors do not form independent realities.
- All sectors belong to one shared environment. The environment precedes the applications contained within it.
- No component shall be elevated above the environment that contains it.

## Article 2 — Interpretations
- Recruiter Mode, Explorer Mode, and Engineer Mode are interpretations of the same Observatory.
- They are not separate products, themes, or independent environments; presentation may differ but truth shall not.
- No interpretation may contradict another; interpretations are layered configurations of a single shared reality.

## Article 3 — Purpose
- Each interpretation exists to fulfill a distinct purpose:
  - Recruiter Mode: establish trust and clarity.
  - Explorer Mode: cultivate presence and discovery.
  - Engineer Mode: reveal structural truth and observability.
- These purposes are complementary and shall coexist within one shared reality.

## Article 4 — Environment
- The Observatory is an environment rather than a collection of pages.
- Navigation exists to reveal topology; applications reveal capability; systems reveal understanding.
- The environment is the primary authority for organization and coherence.

---

Identity anchors the Constitution: implementations and interfaces change, but the Observatory's identity endures as the guiding constraint for architecture and behavior.
