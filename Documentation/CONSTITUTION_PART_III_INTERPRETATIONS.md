# THE MYSTIFY OBSERVATORY CONSTITUTION — PART III: INTERPRETATIONS

This Part defines the three canonical interpretations (modes) of the Observatory and their responsibilities.

## Article 5 — Recruiter Mode
- Recruiter Mode exists to establish clarity, warmth, and trust.
- Its responsibility is confidence, not persuasion.
- Identity precedes capability; capability precedes depth; understanding precedes admiration.
- UX decisions prioritize value extraction, clear flagship projects, and a guided path to contact and resume.

## Article 6 — Explorer Mode
- Explorer Mode exists to cultivate presence and discovery.
- Its purpose is habitation and exploration, not reward or progression.
- Discoveries, entities, and environmental reactions are evidence; presence emerges from evidence.
- UX decisions prioritize layered interactions, contextual hints, and a sense of reactive environment.

## Article 7 — Engineer Mode
- Engineer Mode exists to reveal truth and observability.
- Metrics shall be observable, modeled, or explicitly simulated; the system shall not fabricate telemetry.
- Every surface exposes its epistemological status; the terminal and diagnostics are first-class interfaces.
- UX decisions prioritize inspectability, runtime controls, and high information density.

---

These interpretations are not separate products; they are layered configurations of one Observatory, each tailored to a distinct set of objectives and audiences.
