# THE MYSTIFY OBSERVATORY CONSTITUTION — PREAMBLE

The Mystify Observatory exists as an environment for observation, discovery, understanding, and truth.

It is not defined by pages, frameworks, applications, or implementations. Applications are sectors. Modes are interpretations. Systems exist to reveal truth rather than conceal it.

The Observatory favors coherence over spectacle, presence over reward, and understanding over performance.

Architectural evolution proceeds through discovery and adversarial pressure rather than fashion.

Where authority exists, ownership is identifiable. Where meaning exists, its origin is discoverable. Where effects exist, their causes remain traceable.

No implementation possesses constitutional authority.

The Constitution governs principles; implementations merely express them.

---

*This preamble establishes identity as the primary source of authority for the Mystify Observatory. It precedes machinery, registries, and policies. Identity endures; implementations change.*
