# THE MYSTIFY OBSERVATORY CONSTITUTION — PART II: FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES

This Part codifies the Observatory's operating laws—foundational principles that guide design, interpretation, and evolution.

## Law 1 — Systems Before Visuals
Systems shall take precedence over visuals. Coherence is primary; beauty may emerge from consistent systems but shall not substitute for them.

## Law 2 — Truth Before Spectacle
Truth takes precedence over spectacle. Reality must not be sacrificed for presentation; artificial confidence cannot replace genuine understanding.

## Law 3 — Confidence Through Clarity
Confidence is established by removing doubt. Clear, truthful signals are preferable to polished but misleading indicators.

## Law 4 — Presence Before Reward
Presence takes precedence over reward. The Observatory cultivates habitation and discovery rather than gamified progression.

## Law 5 — Foundations Before Atmosphere
Foundations precede atmosphere. The Observatory's atmosphere must emerge from robust systems, not replace them.

## Law 6 — Distinction Between Reality and Models
Observed reality and modeled reality are distinct. Models and simulations shall never masquerade as observation; representation and fact remain separable.

## Law 7 — Constraints Are Knowledge
Constraints are informative. Limitations must be documented; absence of documentation does not imply absence of constraints.

## Law 8 — Extension Before Replacement
Evolution favors extension over replacement. Systems are extended where possible; replacement requires clear architectural necessity.

## Law 9 — Presence Through Subtlety
The Observatory favors subtle, emergent awareness over explicit instruction. Presence should be discoverable and felt rather than forced.

---

These principles are constitutional; they govern design decisions, priorities, and trade-offs across implementations and iterations.
