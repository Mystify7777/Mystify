# THE MYSTIFY OBSERVATORY CONSTITUTION — PART XI: GLOSSARY

This Glossary stabilizes the Observatory's language by defining terms that express independent architectural distinctions. Language exists to serve architecture; precision takes precedence over familiarity.

## Article 103 — Purpose
The Glossary exists to stabilize architectural distinctions.

Terms exist to express independent topology.

Language serves architecture.

Architecture does not serve language.

Precision shall take precedence over familiarity.

## Article 104 — Consequence
A Consequence is an enforcement act performed by an Enforcement Authority.

Consequences are authoritative.

Consequences are distinct from Outcomes.

Consequences are distinct from Effects.

Consequences belong to the authorities that perform them.

State transitions commonly arise from consequences but do not define them.

## Article 105 — Rendering Authority
A Rendering Authority is the authority responsible for interpreting available information according to owned rules and producing outcomes within its designated domain.

Rendering Authorities own rules.

Ownership of rules transforms computation into interpretation.

Rendering Authorities are distinct from Enforcement Authorities.

Rendering Authorities are distinct from State Domains.

## Article 106 — Effect
An Effect is a downstream result whose existence depends upon a consequence.

Effects possess no authority.

Effects do not originate validity.

Multiple effects may arise from one consequence.

Effects may form chains.

Authority shall not propagate through such chains.

## Article 107 — Outcome
An Outcome is the result rendered by a Rendering Authority.

Outcomes are products of interpretation.

Outcomes precede consequences.

Outcomes are distinct from Effects.

Outcomes are distinct from Outcome Basis.

## Article 108 — Outcome Basis
Outcome Basis is the informational sufficiency foundation upon which an outcome was rendered.

Outcome Basis measures sufficiency rather than truth.

Outcome Basis classifications are:

- Complete
- Partial
- Insufficient

Identical outcomes may possess distinct Outcome Bases.

## Article 109 — Enforcement Authority
An Enforcement Authority is the authority responsible for performing consequences within its designated domain.

Enforcement Authorities own consequences.

Interpretation and enforcement are distinct roles.

Role separation survives implementation co-location.

## Article 110 — Fact
A Fact is information evaluated during outcome production.

Fact is a functional role rather than an ontology class.

Identity remains stable.

Role is positional.

The same information may participate as Fact, Outcome, or Framework at different moments.

## Article 111 — Authoritative Information
Authoritative Information is information whose validity originates within a recognized authority.

Originated validity is the discriminator.

Production by authority alone is insufficient.

Produced information and Authoritative Information are distinct.

## Article 112 — State Domain
A State Domain is the domain owning a distinct class of authoritative state.

State ownership and mutation authority are distinct.

Location does not determine ownership.

Storage does not determine jurisdiction.

## Article 113 — Traversal Class
A Traversal Class is a category of traversal defined by:

- The architectural question being asked.
- The authority responsible for answering it.

Traversal Classes are interrogative rather than topological.

Traversal Classes are distinct from paths.

Traversal Classes are distinct from coordinators.

Traversal Classes are distinct from relationship types.
